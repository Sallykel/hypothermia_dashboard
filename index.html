Newborn hypothermia risk check

I built this from my MPhil thesis. Enter what you know about a newborn on arrival, before the temperature is taken. It gives the model's score and tells you whether the baby would have been flagged in my study.

Not for clinical decisions yet. The model was built from 601 admissions at one hospital and has not been validated elsewhere. Its scores run higher than the true risk. Always measure every baby's temperature and follow your ward's protocol.

    How it works

    The model is a random forest: 400 small decision trees, each trained on a different resample of 480 admissions from my study. Each tree looks at 14 details known at admission and gives a score, and the page averages them. I tested it on 121 admissions it had not seen. At the 0.50 threshold it caught 28 of the 35 hypothermic babies, and it also flagged 21 of the 86 babies who were not hypothermic.

    I gave hypothermic babies extra weight during training so the model would not ignore them. That makes the scores run higher than the real risk, which is why the page shows what happened to test babies with similar scores.
